Consulting

Applied research and measurement work.

I help teams design sound studies and make sense of complex data — survey design and complex statistical analysis, grounded in psychometrics and econometrics.

What I do

Two kinds of work.

Survey & instrument design

Designing surveys and measurement instruments, and participating directly in fielding them — built to measure what you actually intend to measure, not just what’s easy to ask.

Statistical analysis

Complex statistical analysis spanning psychometric methods (item response theory, measurement validity and reliability) and econometric methods (regression-based and causal analysis).

Background

Trained in measurement, not just familiar with it.
