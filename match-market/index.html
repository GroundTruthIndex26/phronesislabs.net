The Match Market

A small, personal way to find the right builder.

Not a freelancer marketplace. The Match Market is a small, relationship-based way to connect people with an idea to vetted creators and builders I know personally — deliberately slow, and personally curated.

How it works

If you have an idea and need someone to build it, tell me about it. I introduce you to a builder I know and trust, chosen because it’s a real fit, not pulled from a directory. If there isn’t a good match yet, I’ll tell you that too, rather than force one.

The same goes in the other direction. I keep a short, personal list of builders I’d actually vouch for, and I only make an introduction when the fit is real on both sides.
