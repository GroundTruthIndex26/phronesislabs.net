The test

Before we build, fund, or back anything, it has to pass both. If either answer is no, we walk away.

Before you write in

Questions people ask before bringing us an idea.

What happens to my idea once I send it?

We read it, run it through the two questions above, and reply with a straight answer — a pass, a pass with conditions, or a no and why. Nothing is built, shared, or shopped around without you.

Do you take equity or ownership?

Only if we build or fund it together, and only on terms we agree to upfront in writing. Ideas that don’t move forward stay entirely yours.

How long until I hear back?

Most ideas get a first response within a few business days. Ideas that move to the Incubate stage take longer, and we’ll tell you what to expect at each step.

What kinds of ideas do you pass on?

Anything that only works by keeping people afraid, confused, or dependent — regardless of how profitable it could be. That’s not a strawman disqualifier; it’s the second question, applied literally.
