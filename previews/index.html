Three ways to open the same page.

One system, three heroes. All of them keep the Phronesis mark exactly as it is, run on the brand navy, cream and orange, and carry the same answer-engine headings now on the live home page. Motion is ambient first: something is always moving, and your cursor and your scroll steer the rest.

The frame drifts, markers pulse, a readout cycles through ideas under review, and a mono strip runs the four stages across the page.

Markers and the floating plate lean toward the cursor. A white panel rides up over the hero as you scroll, and the collage settles into place as it arrives.

Direction 01

The Bench

One cinematic frame, annotated while you watch.

The workbench fills the screen and drifts slowly. A reticle walks across it, reading out ideas as they pass or fail the test, while orange markers pulse over the frame and follow your cursor.

Closest to the reference. Photography carries it.
