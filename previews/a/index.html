How the lab works

How does Phronesis Labs turn a rough idea into something real?

Every idea moves through four stages: think, test, incubate, and send it out. Practical wisdom comes first and the product comes last.

01

Think

We start with the problem and the idea, not the product.

02

Test

Every idea faces two questions. If it only works by keeping people afraid, confused, or dependent, it stops here.

03

Incubate

Ideas that pass get built, funded, or backed, with patience and fair dealing.

04

Send it out

Some become our own ventures. Some go back to the founders who brought them, stronger.

The test

What is the Phronesis Labs test?

Two questions decide everything. Before we build, fund, or back anything, an idea has to pass both.

Question one

Does it leave people better off?

01 / 02
Question two

Does it make money without keeping people afraid, confused, or dependent?

02 / 02

If either answer is no, we walk away.
